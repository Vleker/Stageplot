The short version

StagePlot works without an account. Your shows, input and output lists, stage plots, presets, images and attached files are stored only on your device. We don't use analytics, we don't show ads, and we don't sell or share your data.

1. Information we collect

Feedback you send us

When you use the in-app feedback form, we collect your email address and your message. We use this only to read and reply to your feedback.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. To manage your purchase and let you restore it, we use RevenueCat, which receives a randomly generated anonymous user ID, your purchase and entitlement status, transaction records from Google Play, and basic technical information (device type, operating system version, app version, and country/locale as provided by the store).

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, or the content of your shows. Files you export or share (PDF, CSV, .stageplot) are shared only by you, with whomever you choose.

2. Why we use it

  • To respond to your feedback and support requests (legal basis: your consent and our legitimate interest in improving the App)
  • To provide and restore your purchase (legal basis: performance of a contract)
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as tax and accounting rules

3. Service providers

We use the following providers, which process data on our behalf:

  • Google Play (Google LLC): app distribution and payments
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: purchase management — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Supabase, Inc.: storage of feedback messages, hosted in the EU (Ireland) — supabase.com/privacy

Google and RevenueCat may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, they rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. How long we keep it

Feedback messages and email addresses are kept for up to 12 months after your last contact, then deleted. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide your purchase and to meet legal obligations.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email ClearmindCo@proton.me. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available at this page, with the date at the top.
